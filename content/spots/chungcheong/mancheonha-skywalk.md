The **Mancheonha Skywalk** is a viewing platform with a walkway reaching out about 80 m above the Namhan River (per Danyang County).

## Tickets and hours

Adults pay {{< hl "4,000 KRW" >}}. Youth, children and seniors pay {{< pr "3,000 KRW" >}} (official site). In winter it runs 09:00 to 17:00, and on-site ticket sales end at 16:30 per the county page (some sites say 16:00), so arrive before 3 pm. It is closed every Tuesday unless the Tuesday is a public holiday (official FAQ).

## Getting up

You reach the platform by shuttle bus only. The zipline ({{< pr "30,000 KRW" >}}) and alpine coaster ({{< pr "18,000 KRW" >}}) cost extra.

## Where it fits

In the Danyang 2-night guide it is the early-afternoon stop on Day 2, after Gosu Cave.
