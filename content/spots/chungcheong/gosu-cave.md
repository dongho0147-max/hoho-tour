**Gosu Cave** is a limestone cave registered as Natural Monument No. 256. It is open every day of the year.

## Tickets and hours

Entry starts at 09:00 and the last entry is at 17:00. An adult ticket is {{< pr "11,000 KRW" >}} at the door (list price). Online tickets are listed at 9,900 KRW on the Gosu Cave website.

## Inside the cave

Plan about 1 hour. The cave stays cool all year and parts of the floor are wet, so wear shoes with grip.

## Where it fits

In the Danyang 2-night guide it is the first stop of Day 2, at about 09:00, before the skywalk and the light tunnel.
