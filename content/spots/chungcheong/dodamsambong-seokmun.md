**Dodamsambong** is three rock peaks standing in the Namhan River. It is one of the Eight Views of Danyang. The stone arch called **Seokmun** is nearby, and it often photographs better than the peaks.

## How long and when

Give it about an hour. The light is best in late afternoon.

## The cruise

For the water-level view there is a Dodamsambong cruise. In November and December it runs a shortened route, so check the fare and timetable on the operator's official site (danyangcruise.co.kr). Bring photo ID in case boarding needs it.

## Where it fits

In the Danyang 2-night guide it is the first stop on Day 1, followed by the market and the Jando walkway.
