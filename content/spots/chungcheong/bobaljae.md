Bobaljae is a 540 m pass that links Bobal-ri in Gagok-myeon with Baekja-ri in Yeongchun-myeon. It is also called Godeuneomjae and is part of the Sobaek Mountain foothill road. The road winds for about 3 km, and a viewing deck at the top lets you look down on the bends (Chungbuk Nadeuri, Danyang Geopark).

## When to go

The famous red leaves usually peak around late October, and the dates move each year. On a November to March trip you see bare trees or snow, and the road can ice over, so skip it in rain, fog, or after snow.

## Parking and the view

There is no official car park. Cars use the open ground about 200 m behind the deck, or the roadside, and space is tight in foliage season (Haruharu travel blog). Plan 20 to 40 minutes for the view, and take photos from the deck, not on the bends. Guinsa temple is about 0.8 km further on.

## Where it fits

It is a drive-and-photo stop, not a half-day sight. In the Danyang 2-night guide it is an optional Day 3 morning add-on for people with a car.
