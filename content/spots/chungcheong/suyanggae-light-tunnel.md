**Suyanggae Light Tunnel** is an old railway tunnel turned into a light show, with an LED garden outside.

## Hours and tickets

In winter (November to March) it opens at 14:00 and closes at 21:50. The last ticket is sold at 21:00. Adults pay {{< pr "9,000 KRW" >}}. It is closed on Tuesdays (Suyanggae Light Tunnel website).

## Time needed

Give it 30 to 40 minutes.

## Where it fits

In the Danyang 2-night guide it is the evening stop on Day 2, after Gosu Cave and the skywalk.
