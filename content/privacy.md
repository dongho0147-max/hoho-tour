## Short version

We do not collect your name, email, or any account information. We do not
run ads that track you across the web, and we do not sell data - because we
barely collect any.

## Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It
counts page views without cookies and without collecting personal data. It
does not track you across sites.

If analytics is not yet active on a page, that page simply collects nothing.

## Affiliate links

Some outgoing links are affiliate links (see our
[About page](/about/) for the full disclosure). When you click one and make a
booking, the booking provider may set its own cookies and process your data
under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal information
from those providers - only an anonymized record that a booking happened.

## Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. Embedded services (analytics, booking
providers you click through to) may set their own.

## Children

This site is written for adult travelers. We do not knowingly collect
information from children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
