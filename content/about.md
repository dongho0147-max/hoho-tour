## Who we are

HoHo Tour is a small travel blog run by a Seoul local. We write practical
guides for people visiting Korea for the first time: how to get from the
airport, which subway line to take, where to stay, what to eat, and what is
actually worth your limited days.

We are not a tour company. We do not sell tours, and we do not take
sponsored trips. The guides here are the same advice we would give a friend
landing at Incheon Airport tomorrow.

## How we write

- **We go there first.** A place only makes it into a guide after we have
  actually been there, ridden the subway to it, and eaten nearby.
- **We say when we have not been.** If a recommendation comes from research
  rather than a visit, we label it as such.
- **We keep prices approximate.** Prices in Korea change. We give ranges in
  KRW with a rough USD equivalent and the date we last checked. Always
  confirm before you go.
- **Photos are ours or verified free stock.** We never take photos from
  other blogs. Some guides are text-only until we can photograph the place
  ourselves.

## Affiliate disclosure (FTC)

HoHo Tour participates in affiliate programs, including Travelpayouts. This
means some links on this site - for example links to hotel booking sites,
tours, or eSIM providers - are affiliate links. If you book or buy through
one of them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Affiliate links never decide what we recommend. We only link to services we
would use ourselves, and a commission never changes a price, a rating, or an
itinerary. If a guide contains affiliate links, it says so at the bottom of
the page.
